4 · What's Been Built

The operational overhaul

Seven pillars rebuilt from the ground up since taking over operations.

A

Software & automation

Entire dorm on a cloud PMS — live occupancy, automated check-in/out, payments, dues & analytics on one dashboard.

B

Housekeeping revamp

Team rebuilt twice for quality & reliability, plus floating staff and daily SOPs so 156 beds turn over cleanly.

C

Marketing & outreach

Visiting cards, pamphlets on government roadways buses, 10,000+ flyers locally, and Google Ads targeted at people travelling to Ahmedabad. WhatsApp marketing rolling out next.

D

Online & OTA listings

Listed across major travel platforms (incl. MakeMyTrip) for online discovery beyond walk-ins.

E

Own website — inoutdorm.com

Direct-booking website with navigation guidance — commission-free bookings flow straight in.

F

Group & event bookings

Reached out to multiple event managers to open a bulk group-booking channel — pipeline being built.

G

Local restaurant network

Two-way cross-referral with 4–5 nearby restaurants: they forward stay-seekers to us; we share their menus with guests asking about food.
