HThe Biggest Challenge · Full Transparency

Bedbugs — the one fight still on

Being completely open: bedbugs are currently the single biggest blocker to growth. It has not been for lack of effort — here's everything tried since 2024.

Every approach tried

2024

Bedbugs surface

The issue first appears in the dorm.

Vendor search

4–5 vendors evaluated

Compared chemicals, methods & results before choosing.

2025 · Pro treatment

Vendor + full deep-clean

Beds out, mattresses sun-dried, curtains washed → ~2 mo relief, then recurrence.

Continuous

Weekly 7-day cycle

Kept vendor (Midas) on a recurring schedule — limited lasting effect.

Cost-saving

In-house treatment

Own pump & chemicals; housekeeping treats directly (vendor expensive).

R&D

Powder · silicone · heat-gun

Trialed bedbug-specific powder, silicone and a heat gun.

Feb 2026

15-day shutdown + deep-clean

Closed 15 days, repeated the full deep-clean — colony had multiplied too far.

Honest status

Still unresolved — the colony has multiplied despite everything. A proper, permanent solution is now the #1 priority.

2 full shutdowns7+ methods triedVendor & in-house

Shown openly because solving this is the single fastest lever for occupancy, reviews & growth — and it likely needs a property-level intervention (professional fumigation / structural treatment), not just surface spraying.
