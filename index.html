HThe Biggest Challenge · Full Transparency

Bedbugs — the one fight still on

Being completely open: bedbugs are currently the single biggest blocker to growth. It has not been for lack of effort — here is everything that's been thrown at it since 2024.

Every approach tried

2024

Bedbugs surface

The issue first appears in the dorm.

Vendor search

4–5 pest-control vendors evaluated

Compared their chemicals, methods & results before choosing one.

2025 · Pro treatment

Vendor + full deep-clean

All beds removed, mattresses sun-dried, curtains & everything washed → ~2 months of relief, then recurrence.

Continuous

Weekly 7-day treatment cycle

Kept the vendor (Midas) on a recurring schedule — limited lasting effect.

Cost-saving

In-house treatment

Bought our own pump & chemicals; housekeeping staff treat directly, since the vendor was expensive.

R&D

Powder · silicone · heat-gun

Researched and trialed bedbug-specific powder, silicone and a heat gun.

Feb 2026

15-day shutdown + full deep-clean

Closed the dorm for 15 days and repeated the entire deep-clean — but by now the colony had multiplied too far.

Honest status

Still unresolved — the colony has multiplied despite everything. A proper, permanent solution is now the #1 priority.

2 full shutdowns + deep-cleans 7+ methods tried Vendor & in-house

Shown openly because solving this is the single fastest lever for occupancy, reviews and growth — and it likely needs a property-level intervention (professional fumigation / structural treatment), not just surface spraying.

Lever 1 · The unlock

Solve bedbugs, permanently

A proper property-level intervention (professional fumigation / structural treatment). This alone lifts occupancy, reviews and ratings — and opens the path to ₹8L / month.

Lever 2 · Demand engine

Group bookings from our own database

We already hold contact numbers for many institutes, plus the full phone-number data of every guest who has ever stayed. A messaging campaign to all of them — pushing group & bulk bookings — turns that dormant data into filled beds.
